Lecture at AI4Imaging 2026

Camila was part of the faculty of AI4Imaging 2026 in Maastricht, a four-day hands-on course by Maastricht University's D-Lab on radiomics, deep learning, foundation models and synthetic data for clinicians and imaging researchers. She gave a lecture on out-of-distribution detection, biases and uncertainties: how to tell when an imaging model should not be trusted.
