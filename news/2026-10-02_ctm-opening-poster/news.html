Poster at the opening of the CTM

On 1–2 October, MedUni Vienna opens the Center for Translational Medicine and the Eric Kandel Institute – Center for Precision Medicine, two new buildings on the MedUni Campus AKH that bring experimental laboratories and early clinical trials under one roof. Amir's work was selected for a poster presentation at the Vienna Translational Medicine Conference that marks the opening. Congratulations, Amir!
