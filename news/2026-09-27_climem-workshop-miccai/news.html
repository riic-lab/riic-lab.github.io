Co-organising CLiMeM at MICCAI 2026

Camila is part of the organising team of CLiMeM, the first workshop on Continual Learning in Medical Multimodal-Vision, held on 27 September as a satellite event of MICCAI 2026 at the Strasbourg Convention Center. The workshop asks how medical AI systems can keep learning safely as clinical environments evolve, with seven oral presentations on test-time adaptation, memory replay and continual learning for multimodal medical data, and invited talks by Xiaoxiao Li (UBC) and Anirban Mukhopadhyay (TU Darmstadt).
