Patient-friendly radiology reports in JACR

The paper Sentence-Level Cross-Referencing Improves Comprehension and Confidence in AI-Generated Patient-Friendly Radiology Reports: Design for Understanding, led by Bonnie Armstrong and David Larson at Stanford, appeared in the Journal of the American College of Radiology. In a study with 116 participants, pairing each AI-translated sentence with its source sentence improved comprehension and gave readers the most confidence in the report.
