Adolescent drinking pathways in BP:CNNI

Our paper Divergent Pathways Taken in Adolescence Predict Embracing or Resisting Moderate-to-Heavy Drinking in Young Adulthood, led by Camila with Kilian Pohl's group at Stanford, is out in Biological Psychiatry: Cognitive Neuroscience and Neuroimaging. Using 240 annual measurements from 285 NCANDA participants, a longitudinal deep learning model reveals six developmental pathways that separate stable no-to-low drinkers from moderate and heavy drinkers by age 21.
