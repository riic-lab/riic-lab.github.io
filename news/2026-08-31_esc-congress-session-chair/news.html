Session chair at ESC Congress 2026

At ESC Congress 2026 in Munich, the European Society of Cardiology's annual meeting with almost 34,000 participants and artificial intelligence as this year's spotlight theme, Camila co-chaired the session Artificial intelligence-powered assessment of cardiac function with Tzung-Dau Wang (Taipei): six presentations on AI for echo triage, cardiac CT segmentation, ECG-based risk prediction and explainable valve screening.
