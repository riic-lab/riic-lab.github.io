Sex-specific brain-behaviour mappings

Our paper Regularized CCA identifies sex-specific brain-behavior associations in adolescent psychopathology, led by Leo Milecki with Qingyu Zhao's group at Weill Cornell Medicine, is out in Translational Psychiatry. Analysing resting-state fMRI and symptom scores of 7,892 adolescents from the ABCD Study, sex-stratified canonical correlation analysis reveals distinct brain-behaviour mappings: attention and thought problems are tied to the default mode network in girls but to sensorimotor networks in boys.
