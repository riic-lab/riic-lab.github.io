Organising CoLLAs 2026 in Bucharest

Camila helped organise CoLLAs 2026, the fifth Conference on Lifelong Learning Agents, hosted by Politehnica Bucharest, co-chairing the non-archival paper track with Xialei Liu (Nankai University). CoLLAs brings together research on how machine learning models adapt over time: continual learning, unlearning, model editing and test-time adaptation, from small models to LLMs.
